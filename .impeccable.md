# Design context

Audience: recruiters and engineers inspecting ContractRelay in roughly 30 seconds,
then investigating its adapters, verification evidence and measured limitations.

Jobs: understand the compatibility boundary; compare actual old/new contracts;
inspect persisted plans, gate decisions and execution observations; distinguish
controlled results from historical public migrations without running a model.

Tone: restrained, precise, serious developer tooling. Use readable code, clear
status language, generous spacing and quiet surfaces. Failures receive the same
visual prominence as successful outcomes. No marketing claims or fake metrics.

Direction: a light technical inspection workbench with warm paper surfaces,
ink typography, forest green for verified outcomes and rust for rejection.
Compact navigation and evidence provenance anchor the interface.
